pub async fn connect_grpc_channel(
    addr: SocketAddr,
    tls_config: Option<&GrpcTlsConfig>,
) -> Result<Channel, ClientError>
Expand description

Connect a gRPC channel to addr: in plaintext when tls_config is None, over TLS (or mTLS when a client identity is configured) otherwise.

Errors on an invalid TLS configuration (unreadable certificate material, or a client certificate without its key and vice versa) as well as on connection failure.
